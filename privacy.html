Privacy Policy

Applies to the Laisi Bazaar app and website

Who we are

Laisi Bazaar is a marketplace for buying and selling in Uganda, operated by Alex Nkugwa. Contact: nkugwaalex001@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number and password. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email and profile picture from Google.
  • Listing content: titles, descriptions, prices, location and the photos you upload.
  • Messages: messages you exchange with other users through the app.
  • Usage and device data: pages viewed, saved items, app version, device type and crash reports, used to keep the app working and safe.
  • Payment records: when you pay for a boost, our payment processor handles the card or mobile money details. We do not store your card number.

Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To publish your listings and show them to buyers.
  • To let you and other users contact each other about listings.
  • To process boosts and keep payment records.
  • To prevent fraud, spam and abuse, and to comply with the law.

What other users see

Your listings and your public profile are visible to other users and visitors. Your name, listing photos, location area and contact options are shown with your listings. Your email address and password are never shown to other users.

Sharing

We share data with the service providers that run the app (hosting, database and file storage, email delivery, payment processing), and where the law requires it. We do not sell your personal information.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS. Access to account data is limited to people who need it to run and support the service.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep account data while your account is open. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time using the account deletion page. Some records are kept up to 12 months where the law or fraud prevention requires it.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, or have it deleted by emailing nkugwaalex001@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

Children

Laisi Bazaar is not for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes we update this page and the date below.
