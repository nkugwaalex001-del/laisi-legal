Laisi Bazaar Android app, package name com.ugandadeal.app

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the address you use to sign in, to nkugwaalex001@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Laisi Bazaar account".
  2. Include your account name and phone number so we can find the right account.
  3. We confirm the request within 2 working days and complete deletion within 30 days.
If you signed in with Google, tell us that in the email so we match the right account.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address, phone number, photo and password.
  • Your listings, photos, saved items and messages sent through Laisi Bazaar.
  • Your boosts, referral record and notification settings.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Records we must keep for legal, tax, fraud prevention or dispute reasons are retained for up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Payment or boost receipts held by our payment processor are kept as long as the law requires. Contact them directly for those records.
  • Messages already delivered to other users stay in their inbox, with your name removed.

Before you ask

Deleting your account does not cancel a boost that is still running, and it cannot be undone. You can sign up again with the same email or Google account afterwards, but your old listings will not return.
